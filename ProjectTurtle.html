TURTLES

There are seven different species of sea turtles that roam our oceans, ranging from the Indian Ocean, the Coral Triangle and the Eastern Pacific. They spend most of their lives in the ocean traveling great distances however, they do come ashore to bask or lay eggs. The seven species are: Green, Hawksbill, Loggerhead, Leatherback, Olive Ridley, Kemp’s Ridley and Flatback.



Sea turtles have streamlined bodies compared to their land dwelling counterparts, this gives them the advantage to glide through the water using their flippers to help them guide their path. However due to this, they cannot protect their limbs by withdrawing them to their shells due to the slimmer body shape.
